Clinical Diagnostics: Microbiological & Biochemical Parameters

An extensive 26-page clinical laboratory investigation conducted at Manglamplus Medicity Hospital, Jaipur, and submitted to Jaipur National University. Features detailed clinical workflows in culture media preparation (Blood Agar hemolysis & MacConkey lactose differentiation), bacterial streaking techniques, Kirby-Bauer antibiotic sensitivity testing against CLSI benchmarks, biochemical identification assays (Urease, Citrate, TSI agar), and automated critical care electrolyte quantification (BeckLyte analyzer for Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻, and iCa²⁺).

  • Culture & Isolation: Prepared and evaluated differential and selective media (Blood Agar & MacConkey Agar) utilizing zig-zag and quadrant streaking methods.
  • Kirby-Bauer Antibiotic Sensitivity: Quantified zones of inhibition against standard CLSI charts, analyzing multi-drug susceptibility profiles in clinical isolates.
  • Biochemical Assays: Verified bacterial metabolic profiles using Urease hydrolysis, Simmons Citrate agar, and Triple Sugar Iron (TSI) fermentation.

GPCR Molecular Architecture • Second Messengers • Jaipur National University

G-Protein in Signal Transduction: Molecular Cascades & Disease Pathology

A comprehensive 16-page scientific monograph on the molecular cascades of G-Protein Coupled Receptors (GPCRs) and intracellular G-proteins, submitted to the Department of Biotechnology at Jaipur National University under the guidance of Assist. Prof. Anukriti Pandey. Details 7-transmembrane GPCR conformational activation, heterotrimeric (Gα, Gβγ) and monomeric (Ras superfamily) GTPases, dual second messenger effector cascades (cAMP & IP3/DAG), and clinical pathologies in oncology, cardiology, and endocrine dysregulation.

  • 7TM GPCR Architecture: Modeled 7-transmembrane helix topology, extracellular ligand binding loops, and nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) catalysis.
  • GTPase Switch Mechanism: Traced conformational cycle of Gα subunit dissociation from Gβγ dimer upon GDP-to-GTP exchange and intrinsic GTPase deactivation.
  • Dual Second Messenger Cascades: Analyzed Gs-mediated Adenylyl Cyclase activation producing cAMP and Gq-mediated Phospholipase C cleavage of PIP2 into IP3 and DAG.
